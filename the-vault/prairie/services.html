Our services

Hearing Tests

A thorough, unhurried hearing assessment with your results explained in plain language.

A hearing test the way it should be

Booking your first hearing test can feel like a big step. At Prairie Hearing Westwood you are not a number on a busy schedule. Paige takes the time to understand how you are hearing in the situations that matter to you, whether that is family dinners, the phone, or the quiet of your own home.

Your appointment starts with a friendly chat about your hearing history, any concerns you have noticed, and the moments where hearing feels like hard work. From there we carry out a full assessment in a comfortable, unhurried setting.

What to expect

The assessment is straightforward and completely painless. We check how your ears are working and measure the softest sounds you can hear across a range of pitches. We then walk through exactly what the results mean for you, using plain language rather than jargon.

If your hearing is healthy, we will tell you so. If hearing aids would help, we will explain your options honestly and let you decide in your own time. There is never any pressure to commit on the day.

Why people choose us

Because the same person who tests your hearing is the same person who fits, fine-tunes and looks after you afterwards. That continuity is the whole point of a local clinic.

What to expect

  • A relaxed conversation about your hearing history and lifestyle
  • A full diagnostic hearing assessment
  • Your results explained clearly, with no pressure
  • Honest advice on whether you would benefit from hearing aids

Hearing Aid Fittings

Fitting, programming and fine-tuning so your hearing aids feel right from the very first day.

Fitted properly, by the person who knows your ears

A hearing aid is only as good as the fitting behind it. Getting the right device is the start. Programming it precisely to your hearing, and fine-tuning it as your brain adjusts, is what makes the difference between hearing aids you tolerate and hearing aids you forget you are wearing.

Paige fits each hearing aid to your individual hearing results and to the way you actually live. We take time over the physical comfort too, so they sit securely and feel natural from the moment you leave.

Settling in together

Hearing well again is a gradual process, and small adjustments make a big difference. That is why your fitting includes follow-up appointments. As you wear your hearing aids through real conversations, busy rooms and quiet evenings, we fine-tune the settings so everything sounds as it should.

You will always know who to call, and you will always speak to someone who remembers you.

Brands and styles to suit you

We fit a range of trusted brands and every modern style, from discreet in-canal devices to dependable behind-the-ear models. Take a look at the hearing aids we offer, then book a visit and we will help you choose.

What to expect

  • Hearing aids matched to your hearing and your budget
  • Precise programming to your individual results
  • A comfortable physical fit, checked and adjusted
  • Follow-up appointments to fine-tune as you settle in

Hearing Aid Repairs

Cleaning, servicing and repairs for most makes and models, often while you wait.

Hearing aids you can keep wearing

Hearing aids work hard, every day, in warm and sometimes damp conditions. Over time they need a little care. A blocked filter, a worn tube or a tired receiver can make a good hearing aid sound flat, and it is easy to assume the device is failing when it simply needs servicing.

Bring yours in and we will take a proper look. Many problems are quick to put right, and a lot of repairs can be done in the clinic while you wait, so you are not left without your hearing for days on end.

What we look after

We clean, service and repair most makes and models, including hearing aids that were not originally fitted with us. That covers deep cleaning, replacing tubes and filters, refitting domes and moulds, and diagnosing faults to get your sound back to its best.

If a hearing aid is genuinely beyond an economical repair, we will tell you honestly and talk through your options. No hard sell, just straight advice from someone local you can trust.

What to expect

  • Repairs and servicing for most makes and models
  • Many fixes done in-clinic while you wait
  • Deep cleaning, retubing and fresh fittings
  • Honest advice when a repair is worth it and when it is not

Custom Hearing Protection

Custom-moulded earplugs made to fit you, for work, music, swimming and sleep.

Protection moulded to your ears

Your hearing does not grow back, so protecting it is worth doing properly. Off-the-shelf foam plugs are better than nothing, but they rarely seal well, work loose, and muffle everything equally. Custom hearing protection is moulded from an impression of your own ears, so it fits securely, sits comfortably for hours, and protects without cutting you off from the world around you.

Made for how you use them

We make custom protection for a range of needs:

  • Work and industry, for noisy job sites and machinery
  • Musicians and concert-goers, that lower the volume while keeping music clear
  • Swimming, to keep water out and help prevent ear problems
  • Sleep, for shift workers and light sleepers, or a partner who snores

Taking the impression is quick and comfortable, and we will talk through which option suits your situation. Because they are made to fit only your ears, they stay put and stay comfortable in a way generic plugs never manage.

What to expect

  • Made from a precise impression of your own ears
  • Options for industry, musicians, swimming and sleep
  • A comfortable, secure fit that stays put
  • Far more effective than generic foam plugs
