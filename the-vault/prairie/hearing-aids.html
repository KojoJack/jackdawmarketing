Styles we offer

Every style has its strengths. In your appointment we will narrow it down together, based on your hearing and how you like to live.

Receiver-in-Canal (RIC)

Our most popular style. Discreet, comfortable and suitable for most types of hearing loss, with rechargeable and Bluetooth options.

Best forMost first-time and experienced wearers

Behind-the-Ear (BTE)

Durable and powerful, with the largest battery and the easiest controls to handle. A dependable all-rounder.

Best forMild through to severe hearing loss

In-the-Ear (ITE)

Custom-moulded to fill the outer ear. Easy to insert and remove, with room for larger features and controls.

Best forThose who prefer a single, easy-to-handle piece

In-the-Canal (ITC)

Sits in the lower ear canal for a more discreet look while still being easy to manage day to day.

Best forA balance of discretion and handling

Completely-in-Canal (CIC / IIC)

Our most discreet option, sitting deep in the canal so it is barely visible to others.

Best forThose who want the least visible fit
