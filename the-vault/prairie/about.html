The people behind the care

Meet the team

Paige McCombs

Founder, BC-HIS (Board Certified Hearing Instrument Practitioner)

Hi there! I'm Paige McCombs, founder of Prairie Hearing Westwood.

For over 13 years, I've had the privilege of helping people improve their hearing and reconnect with the moments that matter most. Throughout my career, I've learned that exceptional hearing care isn't just about technology. It is about building relationships, listening to people's concerns, and providing support every step of the way.

That's what inspired me to open Prairie Hearing Westwood: a private, family-owned clinic where patients can expect personalized care, honest recommendations, and the time they deserve. My goal is to create a welcoming environment where every patient feels comfortable, valued, and confident in their hearing healthcare journey.

I graduated from the Hearing Aid Practitioner Program with honors at Grant MacEwan University in 2016. Since then, I have spent my career helping individuals and families find hearing solutions that fit their unique needs and lifestyles. I provide comprehensive hearing assessments, hearing aid fittings, repairs and maintenance, custom hearing protection, and ongoing follow-up care tailored to each individual.

As someone who was born and raised in Winnipeg, I'm proud to serve the community I call home. I look forward to helping you hear your best and experience all of life's important moments with confidence. I can't wait to welcome you to Prairie Hearing Westwood.

Mike Kuchirka

Clinic Operations

Hi! It's Mike Kuchirka.

Growing up in a family-owned contracting business, I learned early that your reputation is built on honesty, hard work, and treating people the right way. Those values have stayed with me throughout my career and continue to guide everything I do today.

Being involved in creating Prairie Hearing Westwood from the ground up has been an incredibly rewarding journey. Watching Paige transform her vision into reality and seeing it come to life has been something I'm incredibly proud to be a part of. I had the privilege of personally building much of the clinic, and together we created a warm, welcoming space where patients can feel comfortable, valued, and confident they're receiving exceptional care.

Whether I'm helping behind the scenes or greeting patients at the front desk, I believe that great service comes from genuine relationships, attention to detail, and a commitment to doing what's right.

I look forward to welcoming you to Prairie Hearing Westwood and helping make your experience a positive one.
