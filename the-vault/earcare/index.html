Services

Hearing care from the first test to years of service.

Everything we do starts the same way: understanding what you're finding hard to hear, and what would make the biggest difference to your day.

Comprehensive ear testing

You'll know the state of your hearing.

You'll get a complete hearing evaluation, so you'll know the state of your hearing and what you can do to hear better.

After the examination we explain the results, so you'll understand your hearing health.

What testing includes

  • Pure tone testing (air and bone conduction)
  • Middle ear testing (tympanometry)
  • Speech testing
  • Otoscopy, to check for wax and other blockages
  • Results explained in plain language, right after your test
  • No charge for hearing tests and assessments

Hearing aids and technology

Matched to your lifestyle and budget.

We carry the latest in hearing aid devices and know the differences between each one. Our goal is to match you with the technology that makes the most sense for you, because when a device fits you're more likely to benefit from regular use.

All major manufacturers

We work with all major hearing aid manufacturers, so the choice is guided by your hearing rather than by one supplier.

Hearing protection

Custom swim plugs and hearing protection plugs, moulded for your ears.

Assistive devices

Amplified phones, bed shakers and fire alarms, whether or not you wear hearing aids.

Inside a current hearing aid: shell, processor, battery and receiver. Device photography supplied by the manufacturer. Confirm image rights and which manufacturers to name

Trials and demonstrations

Try them in your daily routine.

You can try a pair of hearing aids in your daily routine. Bring them home, to work and out in the community, so you can make sure the devices help you hear in the places where you need the most assistance.

We also guide you through the use and maintenance of hearing aids. The trial runs 60 days, there is no charge for it, and if you are not happy you can return the devices for a full refund.

Care for your devices

Included for the life of your devices.

Your hearing devices work better when they are clean, fitted and properly programmed. For the life of your devices we include the following at no charge.

  • Annual hearing evaluations
  • Unlimited cleaning
  • Regular adjustments and programming
  • On-site minor hearing aid repairs
  • Battery program

We honour all manufacturer warranties, even if your devices were not purchased from EarCare Hearing Center.
