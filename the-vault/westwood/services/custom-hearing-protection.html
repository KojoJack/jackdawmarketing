Custom Hearing Protection

Custom-moulded earplugs made to fit you, for work, music, swimming and sleep.

Book a visit (204) 594-9792

Protection moulded to your ears

Your hearing does not grow back, so protecting it is worth doing properly. Off-the-shelf foam plugs are better than nothing, but they rarely seal well, work loose, and muffle everything equally. Custom hearing protection is moulded from an impression of your own ears, so it fits securely, sits comfortably for hours, and protects without cutting you off from the world around you.

Made for how you use them

We make custom protection for a range of needs:

  • Work and industry, for noisy job sites and machinery
  • Musicians and concert-goers, that lower the volume while keeping music clear
  • Swimming, to keep water out and help prevent ear problems
  • Sleep, for shift workers and light sleepers, or a partner who snores

Taking the impression is quick and comfortable, and we will talk through which option suits your situation. Because they are made to fit only your ears, they stay put and stay comfortable in a way generic plugs never manage.

What to expect

  • Made from a precise impression of your own ears
  • Options for industry, musicians, swimming and sleep
  • A comfortable, secure fit that stays put
  • Far more effective than generic foam plugs
