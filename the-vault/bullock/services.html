Our Services

Hearing care for every step of your journey

From your first assessment to years of follow-up visits, here is exactly what we offer and what to expect.

Assessments

Comprehensive Hearing Assessments

A hearing assessment is the starting point for everything we do. It is painless, takes about an hour, and gives us, and you, a clear picture of what is happening with your hearing before we talk about any solutions.

Case History Review

We start by talking through your hearing concerns, lifestyle and any relevant health history.

Full Audiometric Testing

Pure-tone and speech testing measure exactly what you can and cannot hear, across a full range of pitches.

Speech-in-Noise Testing

We test how well you follow conversation in real-world background noise, not just a quiet booth.

Plain-Language Results

Your results are explained clearly, with a copy for your records and no rushed decisions.

Fittings

Hearing Aid Fittings & Programming

If a hearing aid is the right next step, we fit and program the device specifically to your hearing loss, ear shape and daily routine, then verify the fit with real-ear measurement to confirm it is working the way it should.

  • Real-ear verification for an accurate, measured fit
  • A trial period so you can test devices in real life
  • Programming adjusted to how you actually listen

Tinnitus

Tinnitus Support

Ringing, buzzing or hissing in the ears is common, and while there is not always a single cure, there is almost always a way to make it less disruptive day to day.

Tinnitus Assessment

We evaluate the pattern and impact of your tinnitus as part of a full hearing exam.

Sound Therapy Options

Gentle background sound can make tinnitus less noticeable, especially at night.

Hearing Aids with Masking

Many modern hearing aids include features designed specifically to ease tinnitus.

Ear Wax

Ear Wax Management

Excess ear wax is one of the most common, and most fixable, causes of muffled hearing. Our team safely removes built-up wax using methods suited to your ears.

Safe In-Clinic Removal

Wax is removed using gentle, clinically appropriate methods, never cotton swabs.

Same-Day Relief

Many patients notice clearer hearing immediately after their appointment.

Protection

Custom Hearing Protection

Prevention matters as much as treatment. We create custom-molded hearing protection from an impression of your own ears, for comfort and noise reduction that off-the-shelf earplugs cannot match.

Work & Industrial

Custom hearing protection for job sites, farm equipment and other high-noise workplaces.

Hunting & Shooting

Protection built to guard against sudden, high-decibel sound without cutting you off from your surroundings.

Musicians & Concerts

Even-attenuation earplugs that reduce volume while keeping sound quality intact.

Ongoing Care

Ongoing Care & Adjustments

Getting a hearing aid is the beginning of your care, not the end of it. Cleanings, reprogramming and check-ins are included as part of your journey with us.

Routine Cleanings

Regular cleaning and maintenance keeps your devices working at their best.

Reprogramming & Adjustments

As your hearing or lifestyle changes, your devices can be reprogrammed to match.

Annual Hearing Re-checks

Yearly re-assessments help us catch changes early and keep your care plan current.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral to book a hearing test? +

No referral is needed. You can book a hearing assessment directly with our clinic.

How long does a hearing assessment take? +

Most assessments take about an hour, including time to review your results together and answer any questions.

Will my hearing aids be covered by insurance or AADL? +

Coverage depends on your specific plan and eligibility. We can help you understand what Alberta Aids to Daily Living and your private insurance may cover before you commit to anything.

What if I am not ready to buy hearing aids yet? +

That is completely fine. A hearing assessment does not obligate you to purchase anything. It simply gives you clear information to decide from.

Do you see patients from outside Taber? +

Yes, we welcome patients from Taber and the surrounding communities of southern Alberta.

What should I bring to my first appointment? +

Just yourself, and a list of any medications or health conditions worth mentioning. If you currently wear hearing aids, please bring those along too.

Not sure where to start?

A hearing assessment is the simplest way to find out. It is free, painless and comes with no obligation.
