About Us

Care that treats you like a neighbour, not a chart number

Bullock Hearing Centre was built around a simple idea: hearing care should be unhurried, honest and centered on you. We're proud to serve Taber and the surrounding communities of southern Alberta.

Our Approach

Hearing care, without the rush

Many people put off a hearing test because they expect a clinical, impersonal experience. We built our clinic to be the opposite. Appointments are unhurried, the environment is calm and private, and every recommendation is explained in plain language before you decide anything.

  • No pressure to decide on your first visit
  • Every option explained, including the ones that cost less
  • A team that remembers your name, not just your file

What We Believe
