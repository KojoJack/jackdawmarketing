Styles

A style for every hearing loss and preference

Behind-the-Ear (BTE)

A durable, powerful option that sits comfortably behind the ear. Well suited to a wide range of hearing loss, from mild to severe.

Receiver-in-Canal (RIC)

Our most popular style. Small, discreet and comfortable, with excellent sound quality for everyday listening.

In-the-Ear & In-Canal

Custom-molded to sit partly or fully inside the ear canal, for a nearly invisible, glasses-friendly fit.

Everyday Features

Features that make daily life easier

Bluetooth Streaming

Rechargeable Batteries

Cost & Funding

What will it actually cost?

Hearing aid pricing depends on the technology level and style you choose. At your consultation, we'll give you a clear, itemized quote before you decide on anything, along with an honest opinion on which level of technology actually makes sense for your hearing loss.

Many patients are also eligible for support through Alberta Aids to Daily Living, private insurance, Veterans Affairs Canada, or a flexible payment plan through our clinic.
