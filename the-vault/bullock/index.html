Know the Signs

Is it time for a hearing test?

Hearing loss is usually gradual, which makes it easy to miss in yourself and easy to notice in the people around you. If any of this sounds familiar, a hearing assessment is a simple next step.

Turning up the volume

Family members mention the television or radio always seems too loud.

Struggling in groups

Conversations are easy one-on-one but hard to follow at gatherings or restaurants.

Asking people to repeat

You find yourself saying "pardon?" or "can you say that again?" often.

Muffled speech

People seem to mumble, even though others don't have any trouble understanding them.

Ringing or buzzing

A persistent ringing, buzzing or hissing sound in one or both ears.

Avoiding social plans

Skipping calls, visits or events because listening carefully feels tiring.
