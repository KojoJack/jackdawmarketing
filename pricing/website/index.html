Built around your business

Your website should reflect the business behind it.

Every Jackdaw site is designed and developed specifically around your services, customers, brand and goals. The structure, content and customer journey are planned before the site is built so visitors can quickly understand what you do, why they should trust you and how to take the next step.

The result is a professional online presence designed to help turn more visitors into real inquiries.

Fully custom from the ground up

Jackdaw websites are built with code specifically for your business.

This gives us control over how the site looks, loads and functions without relying on a collection of pre-built themes and unnecessary plugins.

Your website is built to be fast, responsive and flexible across phones, tablets and desktops, while giving us a strong technical foundation that can continue evolving as your business and the world around us change.

Built to get found

SEO starts before your website goes live.

Site structure, page titles, headings, content, internal links, metadata, mobile performance and technical accessibility are considered during the build.

Your website is also connected with Google Search Console and Google Analytics so we can understand how people are discovering and using it.

The goal is simple.

Make it easier for search engines to understand your business and easier for potential customers to find it.

More than a website launch

A website should not be built once and forgotten.

Your monthly plan includes ongoing management so the site can change alongside your business.

Need to update a service, replace photos, add a new project, change some text or make a reasonable adjustment?

Send it over.

Jackdaw handles the ongoing website work so you do not need to learn how to manage it yourself or find someone every time something needs changing.

Always improving

Your website can continue getting better after launch.

Jackdaw monitors performance and makes general SEO, technical and website improvements as opportunities appear.

That may include improving page structure, adjusting content, strengthening internal links, improving performance or making changes based on how visitors are actually using the site.

Instead of gradually becoming outdated, your website remains an active part of your business.
