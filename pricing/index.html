The problem

Why most sites underperform

Most small business websites were built once, a few years ago, and never touched again. The design looks dated, the fonts are inconsistent, and it's obvious the business has moved on while the site hasn't.

A lot of them are also slow on phones. Big images, clunky page builders and bolted-on plugins add up, and most visitors are on a phone with an average connection — not a laptop on fast wifi. Slow pages lose visitors before they read a word.

On top of that, most of these sites are hard to find. Google can't rank a site well if the technical basics — page speed, mobile-friendliness, clear titles and descriptions — aren't in place. So even a good-looking site sits invisible on page three.

Why there's a monthly fee

A site isn't a one-time purchase

A website isn't a one-time purchase. Google changes how it ranks pages several times a year, competitors launch new sites, and your own content — prices, hours, projects, offers — goes out of date.

A site with nobody watching it slowly drops in search results and starts showing stale information. That's not a flaw in the build, it's just what happens when nothing is maintained.

The build fee gets you online. The monthly fee is what keeps you found — updates, monitoring and small improvements, so the site keeps working for you instead of quietly falling behind.
