How we work

Run by one person who really likes dirt.

Bramble & Bloom started as a cutting garden in a backyard that got a little out of hand. Now it's a shop, but the habits didn't change. We still grow a third of what we sell, source the rest from growers within a couple hours' drive, and skip the imported roses that spend three weeks in a box before they reach anybody's hands.

If a stem looks a little bent, that's not a mistake. That's just what a flower looks like before someone irons it flat.

“We're not trying to make your flowers look expensive. We're trying to make them look like they're still alive.”
Wren, who started this whole thing
